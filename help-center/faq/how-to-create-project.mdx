## Overview

Creating a new project in ALGONETIC is simple:

1. Log in at `https://dashboard.algonetic.com`
2. Click "New Project" on the home page
3. Enter your project name
4. (Optional) Add a description
5. Click "Create"

Your project will be listed on your dashboard. Click it to organize documentation, invite team members, or configure settings.

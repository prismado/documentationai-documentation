## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to ALGONETIC">
    Go to `https://dashboard.algonetic.com` and log in with your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Click "New Project" and provide a name and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first doc page">
    Inside your new project, select "Add Page" to start documenting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Use "Invite" under project settings to collaborate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

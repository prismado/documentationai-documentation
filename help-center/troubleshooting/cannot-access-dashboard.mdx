---
title: Cannot Access the Dashboard
description: Quick steps if you can't reach ALGONETIC's dashboard.
---

## Symptoms

- Page not loading
- 403 or 404 errors

## Possible Causes

- Browser cache issue
- Expired login session
- Network restrictions

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh your browser">
    Press Ctrl+Shift+R or clear your cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-login">
    Sign out and log in again at `https://dashboard.algonetic.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check network">
    Ensure your firewall allows access to ALGONETIC.
  </Step>
</Steps>
